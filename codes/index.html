About Me

Hello! I'm Alok, an AI/ML Developer and Startup Co-Founder currently pursuing an integrated BS-MS in Science at IISER Kolkata. My work bridges cutting-edge artificial intelligence research with real-world entrepreneurial ventures.

I co-founded UnderWater AI, a government-funded deeptech startup developing computer vision solutions for marine species identification. Our work is backed by MeitY Startup Hub through the GENESIS program, with product launch targeted for 2027.

My research has been published at ECIR 2026, one of the premier conferences in information retrieval. I specialize in large language models, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and building scalable AI systems that solve real-world challenges.
